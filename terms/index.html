Requests, not bookings

Sending the form on this site is a request. Your appointment exists when Jaelene confirms it personally.

Prices and paying

Session prices are the ones shown on the Services page on the day you send your request. You pay at your visit, by cash, check, or card.

Changing or cancelling

Please tell Jaelene before 8:00 AM the day before your appointment if you need to cancel or move it. After that, or if you miss the appointment, a fee of 30% of the session price may be charged. Jaelene looks at each one personally and may waive it. You are asked to confirm you have read this when you send a request.

Gift certificates

Each certificate covers the session it names. Certificates never expire and no fee is ever taken from their value. They are not refundable, and anyone you give one to may use it. If a certificate's value was more than $5 and less than $5 remains, you may ask for the remainder in cash. If you lose a certificate, tell us — we can reissue it once we confirm the purchase and that it has not been used.

Wellness services, not medical care

Healthy Habits provides massage therapy and wellness services. Nothing on this site or in a session is medical advice, and nothing replaces your physician. Jaelene refers anything that needs a physician to a physician. The Personalized Wellness Assessment (EDS) and any supplement suggestions are wellness services offered separately from massage therapy.

Your information and text messages

How we handle your information, and how text messages work, is on the Privacy page.
