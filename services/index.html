Common questions

What should I expect at my first visit?

A conversation first — what brings you in, what your body needs. Every session is fashioned to the individual; Jaelene reads the body to guide each session. Plan a few extra minutes for your first visit.

Do you work with my doctor?

Yes — collaboratively. Jaelene works in conjunction with physicians, naturopaths, and specialists, refers out proactively, and supports post-surgical recovery alongside your medical team with physician clearance.

Is this medical care?

No. Healthy Habits provides wellness services — not medical diagnosis or treatment. Anything that needs a physician's eyes goes to a physician.

How should I prepare for a wellness assessment (EDS)?

Arrive well hydrated. Bring a list of supplements you currently take if you'd like them reviewed.
