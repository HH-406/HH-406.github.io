Swedish Massage

The classic full-body massage — long gliding strokes, warm oil, and moderate pressure. A good first appointment.

Sometimes the right answer is a straightforward massage, and this is that. Long gliding strokes and gentle kneading with warm oil, moderate pressure, an hour of not being responsible for anything.

Swedish is the easiest place to start if you have not had bodywork before, or not for a long time. It is also the honest choice on the weeks when you do not need anything addressed — you just need to put the week down for an hour.

Pressure is a conversation, not a setting. Tell her at the start what you like and tell her during if it changes. Nobody gets a prize for enduring an hour that was too much.
