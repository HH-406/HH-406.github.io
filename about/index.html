About Jaelene

Jaelene Johnson, LMT

A health crisis nearly took Jaelene's life; an innovative treatment saved it — and set the course for her calling: bringing advanced bodywork to rural Montana.

Nearly 40 years and roughly $100,000 of advanced training later — across the US and Canada — she is board-certified in manual lymphatic drainage, bandaging, and compression garments, and was the first to bring lymphatic drainage training to Montana.

She reads the body to guide each session, refers anything that needs a physician's eyes to a physician immediately, and works in conjunction with doctors, naturopaths, and specialists.
